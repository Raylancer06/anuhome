How accurate is Nadi Pariksha (Pulse Assessment)? expand_more

Nāḍī Parīkṣā is a profound classical diagnostic science practiced across millennia. Through tactile radial evaluation at superficial, intermediate, and deep physiological strata, an experienced Vaidya detects subtle organ stress, Ama accumulation, and hormonal fluctuations often before they manifest as macroscopic anatomical pathology.

Can Ayurvedic treatments be integrated with allopathic prescriptions? expand_more

Yes. ANUKUL operates on an evidence-collaborative paradigm. We do not abruptly advise discontinuation of critical allopathic therapies (such as antihypertensives, insulin, or thyroid medications). Our formulations are carefully screened to avoid herb-drug interactions, functioning synergistically to restore underlying tissue health.

Is an Ayurvedic lifestyle feasible for busy professionals and urban living? expand_more

Completely. We strictly avoid rigid or unrealistic dogmas. Our physicians craft realistic, micro-habit Dinacharya frameworks that fit seamlessly into busy modern schedules—focusing on meal timing, digestive tea protocols, simple posture grounding, and realistic home culinary substitutions.

How are herbal formulations verified for purity and safety? expand_more

All herbal preparations dispensed or prescribed by ANUKUL undergo rigorous heavy-metal screening, microbial assay certification, and pesticide residue verification in accredited laboratories, strictly complying with classical pharmacopoeia benchmarks and contemporary international safety standards.
